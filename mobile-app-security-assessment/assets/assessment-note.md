# Optional mobile assessment note

## App and evidence boundary
Platform, package/bundle identity, supplied artifact hashes and provenance,
release/debug/simulator status, OS/runtime, complete or partial installation,
permitted operations, and unavailable material.

## Responsibilities
Product promises; principals; app/OS/SDK/backend decisions; active bindings.

## Material questions
Question | Why this product makes it relevant | Evidence locator | Remaining dependency

## Outcomes
Proposition | Supported / contradicted / inconclusive | Decisive evidence | Limits

## Continue or hand off
Unassessed relevant paths, owner-required material, responsible layer,
changes to earlier conclusions, and facts eligible for independent reporting review.

Do not store secrets, production tokens, unrelated personal data, or private model
reasoning here. A filled note is not certification of a finding or full coverage.
