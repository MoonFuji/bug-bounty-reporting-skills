# Instructor guide: research transfer tasks

Keep this separate from the [learner document](research-transfer.md) for a first
attempt. This is an expected-reasoning guide, not a single mandatory wording or
an automatically computed expertise score. The scenarios are fictional; they do
not reproduce the historical cases described in the source register.

## R1: composition and legitimate projection

Relevant questions arise from final build composition, sharing authority and the
protected distinction between document content and internal notes. R1-B establishes
that a library adds a selected release component. An assessment based only on the
app-authored configuration is incomplete.

R1-C and R1-D support the stated projection/selection property for the finite
cases. The extra component and intentional cross-app sharing are not themselves
violations. Do not certify the entire app from those three checks. R1-E leaves
deployment and behavior of the roadmap extension unresolved; the roadmap alone
cannot establish current exposure.

After the change, Q16 evidence cannot establish Q17 selection. The projection
contract remains a meaningful specification, but its applicability to Q17 must
be re-established. The correct revision is not that Q17 is therefore defective.

Critical errors: treating an SDK or extra component as a vulnerability; ignoring
release selection; inventing internal-note disclosure; treating a roadmap as a
shipped artifact; claiming universal safety from a finite table.

## R2: separate accessibility, capability and temporal permission

R2-A gives an intentional background-availability requirement. R2-B is consistent
with it as configuration evidence; it is not a hardware experiment. R2-C does not
establish the extension's effective signed capability. Do not transfer the app's
signing summary to the extension.

R2-D places authorization and commitment before grant removal. Completion afterward
is consistent with the supplied original contract. R2-E cannot establish a later
authorization or committed effect without correlation. Neither the word accepted
nor absence of a later record proves the server's behavior.

Under the changed cancellation contract, final delivery after removal contradicts
that changed requirement for O7. The observations have not changed; their relation
to the stated policy has. Do not invent a grace period or a universal platform
revocation rule.

Critical errors: declaring every accessible-while-locked item a leak; equating
source entitlements with signed authority; using delivery time as authorization
time; correlating unrelated logs by intuition; transferring a finite result to
all background operations.

## R3: typed interfaces, signatures and eligibility

R3-A establishes separate authenticity, channel/model eligibility and account
requirements. R3-B's types cannot substitute for those rules. R3-C supports the
finite Android decision table, not a universal implementation guarantee.

R3-D establishes a finite contract discrepancy in the supplied iOS adapter path:
authenticity and account ownership do not satisfy the missing channel requirement.
R3-E's integrity result addresses another proposition. Device installation and
physical impact remain unobserved; do not invent them to strengthen severity.

Under the changed scenario, a genuinely authoritative later check can defeat the
end-to-end discrepancy claim even though B's local return is unchanged. Establish
its selection and applicability; do not invent the later check in the original
scenario where the complete trace explicitly excludes it.

Critical errors: accepting signatures or integrity as all-purpose authorization;
presuming Android/iOS parity; calling a typed interface a policy; inventing a
physical effect; failing to revise a conclusion after relevant counterevidence.

## Matched evaluation

Use the same model, tools, evidence, task, budget and output format for baseline
and skill-assisted runs. Repeat them and disclose variance. Human grading should
cover architecture, question selection, applicability, conclusions, and response
to the changed premise. Correct outcome words without the decisive relationship
are insufficient. Alternative priorities are acceptable when grounded.

Do not reward hypothesis volume, severe language, long answers or blanket
inconclusive responses. No learner needs an exploit or external action. Keep
these public exercises distinct from privately held-out transfer projects.
